Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a private tour in Almaty cost?

All tours are priced per car, up to 4 people. Charyn Canyon + Kaindy + Kolsay is $200/car and Golden Man + Esik + Turgen is $150/car — both full-day private guided tours. Medeu + Shymbulak is a private car transfer for $100/car, cable car tickets paid on site. The all-inclusive 3-day package (all three tours + round-trip airport transfer) is $450/car.

What tours are available in Almaty?

Three main private tours — Charyn Canyon + Kaindy + Kolsay, Golden Man Museum + Esik Lake + Turgen Waterfall, and Medeu + Shymbulak. We also offer a 3-day all-inclusive package, plus custom tours on request (airport transfer, Kok Tobe, city walks, food tours, photography tours, multi-day trips).

How do I book a tour?

Simply contact us via WhatsApp at +7-706-720-4680, or click any "Book this tour" button to start a pre-filled chat. We respond within 15 minutes and help you plan the perfect itinerary.

Do you offer airport transfers?

Yes. Round-trip airport transfer is bundled into our 3-day package, and standalone airport pickup or drop-off is available as a custom add-on — just send us your flight details on WhatsApp for a quick price.

What's the maximum group size?

All tours are private with a maximum of 4 people per car. You set the pace and never share the vehicle with strangers.

Is the tour guide English-speaking?

Yes, our guide speaks English and provides personalized, friendly service for all international travelers visiting Almaty.

What is included in the tour price?

Full-day guided tours (Charyn + Kaindy + Kolsay, Golden Man + Esik + Turgen) include private car/SUV transport, English-speaking guide, hotel pickup and drop-off, and national park or museum entrance fees. The Medeu + Shymbulak tour is a private car transfer only (driver, no walking guide) — cable car tickets are paid on site. Food and personal expenses are not included unless stated otherwise.
